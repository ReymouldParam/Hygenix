R & D

At Hygenix Seeds, we believe that and the long-term success of our business is rooted in a strong commitment to Research and Development. We prioritize substantial investments in R&D and technology to develop innovative, high-performing products.

To accelerate our research process, we’ve collaborated with the World Vegetable Center in Taiwan, a global leader in providing Germplasm lines. These lines provide hybrids with high yields, and strong tolerance to diseases and pests. Once identified through our R&D process, these hybrids undergo rigorous testing across multiple locations before being commercialized for the market.

Hygenix Seeds utilises Bio-tech tools in the YVR group’s Molecular Breeding Lab. Our highly skilled team of scientists carries out advanced Molecular Breeding activities, ensuring that we stay at the forefront of agricultural innovation. With these strategic investments in breakthrough technologies, we are confident in our ability to introduce winning vegetable hybrids to the market.
