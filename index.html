Welcome to Hygenix Seeds

Welcome to Hygenix Seeds, where innovation meets agricultural excellence. With Hygenix Seeds, farming starts with the right seed. Our high-yielding, disease-tolerant vegetable hybrids are designed to help farmers succeed in every field and every season, across the country.

Founded in 2022 by Shri Yaganti Venkateswarlu, Hygenix Seeds is driven by a clear purpose, to enrich farmers' lives by delivering superior seeds. Our specialized breeding programs create hybrids tailored to India's diverse agro-climatic zones ensuring better harvest.

From our state-of-the-art research facility in Hyderabad, we fuel innovation with a dynamic pipeline of over 50 breakthrough varieties in major vegetable crops. With every seed, we are committed to delivering stronger resilience, higher yields, and lasting prosperity for farmers.
